What is Odyssey?

Odyssey is a personal WhatsApp gateway that turns your WhatsApp into a unified command center. Instead of juggling multiple apps, you get everything through one conversation.

It connects to your WhatsApp using an intelligent routing engine. When you send a message, Odyssey figures out which of your services should handle it, whether that's saving a quick note, creating a task, or setting a reminder.

Built with an AI-powered intent classifier, Odyssey understands context. No command needed for follow-ups. It remembers what you were doing and routes your next message to the right place automatically.

How it works

💬

You message

Send any message to your Odyssey WhatsApp number

🧠

Odyssey routes

The intelligent router matches your intent to the right service

⚡

Service responds

Your app handles the request and replies back on WhatsApp

Four-tier intelligent routing

T0
Local commands
/remind, /help: handled instantly, no external service
T1
Explicit commands
/task, /keep, /done: fast-path to your service
T2
Session continuity
Follow-up messages auto-route to your last service
T3
AI classification
Natural language gets classified and routed automatically

Command Center

Full reference for every command available in Odyssey.

/remind

Set a one-off reminder using natural language. Fires once at the specified time.

Keywords

/remind today Buy milk
/remind tonight Call mom
/remind tomorrow Dentist at 10am

Relative durations

/remind in 30 min Check the oven
/remind in 2h Stand-up meeting
/remind in 3 days Follow up with client
/remind in 2w Book flights

Weekdays

/remind monday Submit the report
/remind this friday Team dinner
/remind next wednesday Review PRs

Named dates

/remind 25 sep Book flight
/remind 1 jan New year goal setting

Numeric dates

/remind 25/09 Dentist appointment
/remind 2026-09-25 Conference

Bare times

/remind 10am Standup
/remind 1:30pm Lunch with Sarah
/remind 13:30 Call bank

Combined

/remind tomorrow 5pm Review PRs
/remind monday 9am Sprint planning
